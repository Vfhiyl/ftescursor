- **ASCO Post（2026-07）**：[EMERALD-3 Shows Benefit With STRIDE Plus TACE](https://ascopost.com/news/july-2026/emerald-3-shows-benefit-with-stride-plus-tace-in-hepatocellular-carcinoma/)

- 摘要 DOI：[10.1200/jco.2026.44.17_suppl.lba4000](https://doi.org/10.1200/jco.2026.44.17_suppl.lba4000)
