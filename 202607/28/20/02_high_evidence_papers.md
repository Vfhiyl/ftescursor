## A. PubMed 精选（临床优先）

### Atezolizumab Plus Bevacizumab Versus Durvalumab Plus Tremelimumab for Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma: A Propensity-Score-Matched Analysis.

- **标签**: Real-world head-to-head / PSM（本小时最重要临床比较）
- **PMID**: [42512110](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42512110/)  |  **期刊**: Biomedicines
- **DOI**: [10.3390/biomedicines14071638](https://doi.org/10.3390/biomedicines14071638)
- **解读**: TriNetX（112 机构 EHR）识别一线 A+B（n=2031）与 D+T（n=788）；PSM 后 **1536** 例纳入生存分析。中位 OS **16.3 vs 22.5 mo，HR 0.89（95% CI 0.76–1.0），无显著差异**；D+T 免疫介导结肠炎更高。结论：两方案均为合理一线，可按合并症、不良反应与偏好个体化。
- **兼听**: 与 Mayo 六中心 RW、早期 TriNetX 比较、Frontiers 目标试验模拟等结果方向一致（OS 大体相当；个别队列见 A+B PFS 更长或 1 年 OS 数值优势）。**非随机、残余混杂仍在**——不可单独改写指南排序。

### Periductal Fibrosis and Cholangiocarcinoma-Related Outcomes in Liver Fluke-Endemic Regions: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

- **PMID**: [42506349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42506349/)  |  **期刊**: Medical sciences (Basel)
- **DOI**: [10.3390/medsci14030380](https://doi.org/10.3390/medsci14030380)

### Does Acute-on-Chronic Liver Failure Still Matter After Liver Transplantation? A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of One-Year Survival.

- **标签**: Systematic review / Meta-analysis（移植）

### Prognostic factors and treatment response in previously treated hepatocellular carcinoma patients receiving Y-90 radioembolization combined with targeted-immunotherapy: an exploratory analysis.
