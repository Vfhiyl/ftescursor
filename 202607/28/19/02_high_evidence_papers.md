## A. PubMed 精选（临床优先）

### Periductal Fibrosis and Cholangiocarcinoma-Related Outcomes in Liver Fluke-Endemic Regions: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.

- **标签**: Systematic review / Meta-analysis（本小时最高证据形态）
- **PMID**: [42506349](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42506349/)  |  **期刊**: Medical sciences (Basel)
- **DOI**: [10.3390/medsci14030380](https://doi.org/10.3390/medsci14030380)
- **解读**: 肝吸虫流行区超声定义的导管周围纤维化（PDF）与胆管癌相关结局；汇总患病率与关联 OR。纳入研究少（5 项），外推需谨慎，但对肝胆外科地方病区筛查有公共卫生价值。

### Prognostic factors and treatment response in previously treated hepatocellular carcinoma patients receiving Y-90 radioembolization combined with targeted-immunotherapy: an exploratory analysis.

- **标签**: Clinical cohort / Y-90 + TT + IT
- **PMID**: [42517902](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42517902/)  |  **期刊**: Abdominal radiology (New York)
- **解读**: 既往治疗过的 HCC 再接受 Y-90 联合靶免——探索性、样本量小（n≈30）、OS 事件少。与桥接/降期及 NCT07059494（Y90+Atezo/Bev）登记更新同日呼应；**不可替代 III 期**。
- **注**: 条目 DOI 字段疑似错配，以 PMID 为准。

### Real-World Outcomes of Combined Carbon-Ion Radiotherapy and Systemic Immunotherapy for Hepatocellular Carcinoma (Atezolizumab Plus Bevacizumab or Durvalumab Plus Tremelimumab): A Single-Center Retrospective Study.

- **标签**: Real-world / CIRT + IO
- **PMID**: [42513363](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42513363/)  |  **期刊**: Journal of clinical medicine
- **DOI**: [10.3390/jcm15145449](https://doi.org/10.3390/jcm15145449)
- **解读**: 碳离子放疗联合 Atezo+Bev 或 Durva+Treme 的真实世界测序（n=41）——对接日本粒子线保险适用语境；证据等级低于 III 期。

### Immunotherapy-Based Conversion to Curative-Intent Treatment in Hepatocellular Carcinoma: A Multidisciplinary Framework.

- **标签**: Multidisciplinary framework
- **PMID**: [42512302](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42512302/)  |  **期刊**: Cancers
- **DOI**: [10.3390/cancers18142234](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers18142234)
- **解读**: 免疫为基础的转化至根治意图治疗框架——强调影像缓解≠根治候选；亚洲“转化切除” vs 西方“移植降期”术语差异。对接中国 2026 指南转化强化方向。

### Prevalence and Cure of Primary Liver Cancer in Italy by Histologic Type.

- **标签**: Population statistics (Italy)
- **PMID**: [42512383](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42512383/)  |  **期刊**: Cancers
- **DOI**: [10.3390/cancers18142318](https://doi.org/10.3390/cancers18142318)
- **解读**: 31 个意大利癌症登记处（覆盖约 47% 人口）——按组织学估计完全患病率与治愈分数；HCC 优于 ICC 的长期结局对照。

### Prediction of Long-Term Prognosis in Patients With Hepatocellular Carcinoma Using the National Clinical Database Risk Calculator.

- **标签**: National registry prediction
- **PMID**: [42516535](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42516535/)  |  **期刊**: Annals of gastroenterological surgery
- **DOI**: [10.1002/ags3.70256](https://doi.org/10.1002/ags3.70256)
- **解读**: 日本 NCD 风险计算器预测肝切除后长期预后（n=210）；预测死亡率 ≥3% 与更差 RFS/OS 相关。外推需外部验证。

### Prognostic Significance of Combined α-Fetoprotein and PIVKA-II for Hepatocellular Carcinoma After Hepatectomy.

- **标签**: Biomarker prognosis / surgery
- **PMID**: [42504321](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42504321/)
- **解读**: 术前 AFP（>400）+ PIVKA-II（>40）双阳性分层术后 OS/RFS（n=553）——外科预后工具，非治疗试验。

### CRAFITY and PALBI Define a Machine Learning-Supported Prognostic Framework in Hepatocellular Carcinoma.

- **标签**: Prognostic scores / ML
- **PMID**: [42505562](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42505562/)
- **解读**: CRAFITY（IO 语境）与 PALBI 结合机器学习扩展 BCLC 之外的预后框架；需外部验证后再用于 MDT。

### Where does histotripsy fit into hepatocellular carcinoma care?

- **标签**: Technology review
- **PMID**: [42504148](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42504148/)  |  **期刊**: Cancer
- **解读**: FDA 批准的非热消融器械在 HCC 路径中的定位；强调对照疗效、比较有效性与安全性证据缺口——**勿当标准疗法外推**。

### Interventional Radiology in the Management of Primary Liver Malignancies. / Intraoperative Ultrasound in Hepatic Oncology Surgery.

- **标签**: Narrative reviews（局部治疗 / 外科技术）
- **PMID**: [42512347](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42512347/), [42512372](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42512372/)
- **解读**: 介入局部治疗全景与术中超声对手术策略的影响——教学/综述价值高，非新 III 期数据。

### Advances in Immunotherapy for Intrahepatic Cholangiocarcinoma.

- **标签**: iCCA immunotherapy review
- **PMID**: [42511572](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42511572/)
- **解读**: iCCA 免疫微环境与 IO 进展综述——对肝胆外科 MDT 中不可切除 iCCA 系统治疗背景有用。
